About the library

OpenLutheranLibrary publishes Lutheran books as new, freely available editions.

Every work is kept as LaTeX and typeset with LuaLaTeX. The PDF is the typeset book itself; the online reader and EPUB are produced from the same source by TeX4ht, so headings, footnotes, margin notes and the source’s page references follow the LaTeX. They are new editions, not facsimiles of the original printings. The LaTeX source of every work can be downloaded from its page.

The transcriptions start from OCR of the original printings and are corrected by hand; uncertain readings are marked.
