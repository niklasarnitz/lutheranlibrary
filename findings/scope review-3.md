# Evangelisch-Lutherischer Gebets-Schatz (1881): OCR review 3

## Scope and disposition

Reviewed Folio scans 375–559 (185 scans) for missing-text/refusal entries and the requested structural and reading issues. The range has 177 pages with OCR text and eight empty entries: scans 452, 471, and 472 are AI refusal blanks and are restored in `review-3.json`; scan 479 is genuinely blank; scans 556–558 are blank or too damaged for readable text; scan 559 is the cover. Scans 552–555 contain engraved melody scores and should remain scan facsimiles, as requested; their syllabified OCR is not proposed for publication.

No published source, live Folio record, or source scan was edited. Corrections and restored text are in the review JSON only.

## Missing pages restored

- **452:** Completes the visible end of the preceding hymn stanza, then hymn stanzas 39 and 40. The hymn stanzas are paragraphs, not line-by-line blocks; the running header, folio, and Google watermark are excluded.
- **471:** Restores the continuation of the Lord’s Prayer beginning with the visible `unser!` (the preceding scan ends `Vater-`), followed by the opening of “479. Eine kurze Litanei nebst Gebeten bei einem Sterbenden.” The printed response groupings are kept together.
- **472:** Restores the litany continuation, the Lord’s Prayer, “Gebet.”, “Desgleichen.”, and “480. Sterbensseufzer.” The short verse remains one paragraph.

## Source-verified corrections

- **440:** The scan reads `Abbaum`; the OCR uncertainty marker is removed without normalizing this unusual printed form.
- **454:** Prayer 460 is titled “Gebet eines bußfertigen Kranken.”
- **478:** The title page reads “Hausgesangbüchlein.”, not “Hausgelaßbüchlein.”
- **484:** The printed word is `annoch`; the Greek-looking `ανnoch` is an OCR error.
- **500:** The two-column reading sequence is blocks **0–4, 11–13, 5–10, 14–16**. The correction reorders the block 11–13 hymn before its continuation in blocks 5–10. The bylines read “Joh. Walther.” and “E. G. Woltersdorf.” The broken two-line stanza fragment is joined within its paragraph.
- **534:** The scan reads `ströhmt` in stanza 8. Stanza 15 visibly divides `gotts-` / `sel’g` at the line end; the correction records that printed division as `gotts-sel’g` rather than completing or modernizing it.
- **507:** The token after “und träget” remains unresolved. The scan is not clear enough to replace the existing uncertainty marker safely; no reading has been inferred from a remembered hymn version.
