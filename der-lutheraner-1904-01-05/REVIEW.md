# Editorial review — Der Lutheraner, 5 January 1904

Volume 60, issue 1, St. Louis. All 16 scan pages are represented in the
complete edition, including the masthead and publication notices. The issue
contains 42 separately catalogued, topic-tagged articles and administrative
rubrics. District accounts and institutional donation reports remain grouped;
individual donations are not treated as articles.

## Source and method

- Original: [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/download/der-lutheraner_1904-01-05_60_1/der-lutheraner_1904-01-05_60_1.pdf).
- Original PDF SHA-256: `6a8e1c0f40e55fd30f1309d0d9d8580f06e2e8ad66e079a4e23165ba85824cdd`.
- Transcription began with the latest completed Folio page revisions for
  document `287752b69c5947e5b36a0309b2985932`, not a new OCR run.
- Three GPT-6 Luna reviewers compared pages 1–6, 7–12 and 13–16 with the
  scan. Their fragments were assembled and checked editorially.
- Historical spelling, abbreviations and printed monetary totals are retained.
  Totals have not been recalculated. Running heads and folios are omitted;
  `\quellseite` records scan-page boundaries, not newly typeset page numbers.
- Complete issue and individual editions input the same article bodies, so
  corrections cannot diverge between those editions.

## Material corrections

### Pages 1–6

Restored “keinen seiner Leser” in the Vorwort: its omission reversed the
meaning. Other scan-verified readings include “kein reiner Segen,”
“P. Aug. C. Senne,” “Segenspender,” “Mitchristen,” “mir sagen,” and
“alle Christen.” Joined the page-break hyphen in “geworden” and restored
the printed abbreviation `\&c.`.

An initial review retained “den Freunden zurechthelfen.” A high-resolution
follow-up comparison, confirmed by that reviewer, corrected this to
**“den Irrenden zurechthelfen.”** The latter is the integrated reading.

### Pages 7–12

Corrected “viele dahinlautende Aussprüche” (not “Zehntausende”), the
chorister payment **$2000.00** (not $20000.00), and both page-7 bylines
to **L. F.** (not Q. F.). Further corrections include “bestieg,” “unirte,”
“tätowirt,” “Aschgraue,” “Zitternd,” “Niagarafalles” and “amtirte.”
Literal dollar signs are escaped for LaTeX.

### Pages 13–16

Corrected financial rubrics and abbreviations including “Kass.,”
“Negermission,” “Dankscoll.,” “Erntescoll.,” Kansas “Synodalbaukasse”
and “Emigrantenmission.” The scan reads **“Bühler-Bund,”** not
“Bühlers-Bund” or the proposed “Luther’s-Bund.” Salt Lake City's
California/Nevada receipt is **$5.00**, and the Minnesota/Dakota
Heidenmission subtotal is **$6.75**. These are transcription corrections,
not recalculated totals.

The deaf-school report's verified heading is “Erhalten an Liebesgaben für
die Taubstummenanstalt zu North Detroit, Mich”. Other corrections include
“11 S. Hafer,” “2 Gänse,” “Fr. Taube 1 Schinken,” “Kostgeld,”
“Festcollekte” and “Zeitungsbriefe.” Financial subheadings consistently
use `\section`. The Illinois page-14 marker precedes “Innere Mission im
südlichen Illinois”; its preceding list continues across page-13 columns.

## Article boundaries and titles

“Statistisches Jahrbuch” (page 6) and “Religiöser Unterricht der Jugend”
(page 7) have editorially supplied titles. The yearbook notice ends with
its own byline; the education report is a separate item, not a continuation.
“Christliche Heilwissenschaft” is separate, without duplicated text.
Short notices are catalogued under their printed rubrics.
